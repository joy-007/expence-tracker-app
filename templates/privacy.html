Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect basic details such as your name, email address and password. As you use Spendly, we also store the expense information you enter, including amounts, categories, dates and descriptions. We do not collect more than is needed to provide the service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to run your account, record your expenses, and show you summaries and breakdowns of your spending. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your expense data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored for as long as your account is active. We take reasonable steps to protect it from unauthorised access, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You may ask us to delete your account and the data associated with it at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser may connect to Google's servers when you visit the site. We do not share your expense data with third parties. If we add other services in the future, we will update this policy to describe them.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request deletion of your data, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
